Privacy policy

Sextant · Effective September 23, 2026

Sextant is a private tool operated for J.W. Mitchell, Inc. and its owner, Jerald W. Mitchell (“the owner”). It is used only with QuickBooks Online companies that belong to the owner. This policy explains what it reads and how that information is handled.

Information the app reads

With the owner’s permission, the app reads accounting information from the owner’s QuickBooks Online companies: financial reports (for example Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Trial Balance, General Ledger and transaction lists), lists (accounts, customers, vendors, classes, departments and items) and basic company information. It does not collect information from anyone else, and it has no public sign-up.

How it is used

Solely to keep the owner’s own organized record of his books and to help the owner and his family review them. The information is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Where it is kept

Copies are stored in the owner’s private files on the owner’s computer and the owner’s private network storage. The credentials that allow the connection are kept in the computer’s encrypted system keychain, not in those files. The app itself does not send QuickBooks information to any outside service.

AI assistants

The owner may review his saved records with his own AI assistants (OpenAI’s ChatGPT and Anthropic’s Claude). When he asks them a question, they read the relevant records, including QuickBooks copies, to answer it, under those providers’ own terms. They only read; they cannot change anything in QuickBooks.

Who can see it

The owner and the family member who maintains the owner’s records. Nobody else is given access.

Keeping and deleting

Copies are kept as part of the owner’s records until the owner deletes them. The owner can stop all further reading at any time by disconnecting the app in QuickBooks Online.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions about it can be directed to J.W. Mitchell, Inc.
