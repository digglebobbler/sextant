Sextant

A private, read-only QuickBooks connection.

Sextant is a private record-keeping tool. With the owner’s permission, it reads accounting reports from the owner’s own QuickBooks Online companies and keeps copies in the owner’s private files, so the owner has a complete, organized history of his books.

What it does

  • Reads reports such as Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, General Ledger and transaction lists, plus lists of accounts, customers and vendors.
  • Only reads. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks.
  • Serves only the owner’s own companies. It is not offered to the public or to other QuickBooks customers.

Connecting

The owner connects a company once from the owner’s own computer. QuickBooks shows a consent page, and the connection starts only after the owner signs in and chooses “Connect”.

Disconnecting

The owner can disconnect at any time in QuickBooks Online (the Apps section of the company settings). Copies already made stay in the owner’s private files unless the owner deletes them.
